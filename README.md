# AI Evaluation Samples

## Overview

This repository contains sample evaluations of AI-generated Japanese responses.

The purpose is to demonstrate practical skills in reviewing Japanese language quality, naturalness, clarity, tone, localization accuracy, and contextual appropriateness.

---

## Evaluation Focus

The following aspects are reviewed:

- Natural Japanese expression
- Grammar accuracy
- Tone consistency
- Clarity and readability
- Cultural appropriateness
- Translation quality
- Localization suitability
- User understanding

---

## Example Evaluation Workflow

### Step 1: Review AI Output

Identify unnatural wording, mistranslations, unclear phrasing, or awkward tone.

### Step 2: Explain the Issue

Describe why the sentence feels unnatural or incorrect for native Japanese users.

### Step 3: Provide Improved Japanese

Rewrite the sentence into more natural and contextually appropriate Japanese.

### Step 4: Final Quality Check

Confirm:
- readability
- consistency
- natural tone
- user friendliness

---

## Example Review Categories

### 1. AI Translation Review

Comparing original English text and AI-generated Japanese.

### 2. Naturalness Improvement

Improving robotic or machine-like Japanese expressions.

### 3. Tone Adjustment

Adjusting overly direct or unnatural tone into natural Japanese communication.

### 4. Contextual Accuracy

Checking whether the meaning matches the intended context.
